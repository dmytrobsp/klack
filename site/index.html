Get it

One zip per system, one binary inside. Unzip it, put klack on your PATH, sign in:

$ klack auth login

SHA256SUMS · release v0.8.0

  • None of them is signed, so your OS will have opinions. macOS: xattr -d com.apple.quarantine klack once, or allow it in System Settings → Privacy & Security. Windows: SmartScreen may ask once.
  • Video, cameras and screen sharing want ffmpeg on your PATH (on Wayland, screen sharing goes through the desktop's own picker and GStreamer).
  • Huddles are at home on Linux. On macOS and Windows your camera and screen sharing are written but not yet tried for real — they should work; if they don't, tell us.
