What it does

Huddles and screen sharing. In a terminal.

Join, talk, share your screen and watch theirs — tiles, video and all, drawn right in the terminal. You join muted; the mic opens only when you say so.

The official app shares your screen and heats your room. We skipped the heating. See the numbers.

Sign in with one code.

Scan the QR code with your phone, type the code from your email, done. No browser needed on this machine. (Your browser's session or a pasted cookie work too — the guide offers both.)

Opening your browser to sign in… The official app is a browser, and it still sends you to another one.

Hands on the keys.

NORMAL and INSERT modes, like vim. j/k, g/G, Ctrl+U/Ctrl+D. Space is the leader: press it, wait a beat, and the which-key panel says what comes next. Every key rebindable. The mouse works too, if you must.

The official app's idea of a modal editor is a modal dialog.

:q

Quit with Ctrl+Q. We're not monsters.

Script it: klack ctl.

The same Slack, from your shell: whoami, channels, history, replies, post, react, pin, upload, search, listen… Pipe it, cron it, wire it into CI.

The official app's automation story: drive a browser with another browser. Ours is a pipe.

● ● ● sh
$ make test && klack ctl post '#deploys' 'green. ship it.'
$ klack ctl history '#deploys' | grep -i rollback
$ klack ctl listen | grep -i outage

And the rest

  • threads a reply pane, and every thread you're in, unread first
  • reactions the picker even says who reacted
  • custom emoji as pictures, where the terminal can draw them
  • pictures in the terminal kitty, WezTerm, Ghostty, foot, any sixel terminal — half-blocks elsewhere
  • video with sound Space pauses. Obviously.
  • files upload, paste a screenshot, save attachments
  • search more results load as you scroll
  • notifications on your desktop, with a sound if you want one
  • do not disturb /dnd, a pause, a schedule
  • several workspaces a rail of tiles, one keystroke apart
  • themes dark, light, terminal, or your own TOML
  • links into your Slack open in klack, not in a browser tab
  • drafts half-written messages survive quitting and crashes
  • slash commands Slack's and the apps' — /remind, /giphy, /shrug
  • your keys rebind anything; your keys.toml keeps its comments

All of it in one terminal tab. Not a Chromium in sight.

The numbers

Same machine, same session, one app at a time. Measured, not guessed.

The official app ships a whole browser to show you a list of channels. It shows.

measuring… no number goes up here until it's been measured.

Everything here was measured on Linux. macOS and Windows numbers? Sorry, can't hear you over the sound of my freedom.

Chat over SSH: yes. Huddle over SSH: only if you shout really loud.

SSH forwards ports, not vocal cords.

For the box with no screen and no browser: run klack auth login in the SSH session, scan the QR code with your phone, type the code from your email. Signed in.

Then it's all there: channels, DMs, threads, reactions, search — in the terminal you already have open.

The official app over SSH: forward X, drag a whole Chromium down the wire, go make coffee. Grow the beans first.

Get it

One zip per system, one binary inside. Unzip it, put klack on your PATH, sign in:

$ klack auth login

No installer. No auto-updater. No herd of “Slack Helper (Renderer)” processes.
